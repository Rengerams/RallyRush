# Rally Rush 0.3.0 — a bigger view, better rallies

**Status:** Release notes draft. The Windows build is working; clean-install and upgrade checks for Windows 10/11 still need to be recorded before publication.

Ready for another set? Rally Rush 0.3.0 makes it easier to get on court, find your rhythm, and see how the match is going. Here’s what’s new.

## Find your feet, then find your game

New to Rally Rush? A short, skippable first-session guide walks you through movement, sprinting, sliding, shot choice, timing, and the scoreboard. You can replay it from Setup or Pause whenever you want a refresher. Clearer control hints help you get into the action without guessing.

And when you want to warm up before the big match, head to Practice:

- **Timing drill:** return balls and sharpen your release timing, from Miss to Perfect.
- **Serve practice:** work on the toss and strike, with feedback when a serve goes wrong.
- **Target practice:** pick a court lane and aim your shots there.

Practice is for getting better—drill attempts stay out of your match history and leaderboard.

## The court takes center stage

The match view gives the court and stadium more room, especially in a wide desktop window, while keeping the action framed. The score is easier to follow too: see points, games, sets, and the server between rallies, with a compact score display while the ball is in play.

Setup is quicker with editable quick-play presets for the surface, AI style, difficulty, and match format. They’re starting points, so you can still tune your player and make the match your own.

Keyboard, mouse, and touch controls get clearer in-game hints. The interface also brings better keyboard focus, contrast, and screen-reader announcements for score changes and match calls, plus reduced-motion support for callouts.

## Rallies with a little more rhythm

Movement has been tuned to feel more deliberate: players accelerate, turn, slide, and recover more consistently, and slides ease to a stop more naturally. Stamina recovery now distinguishes standing still from walking, so taking a breather has a clearer feel.

Contact, spin, and court-surface bounce have been tuned alongside gameplay stability. AI difficulty has also been calibrated and checked across repeatable matchups, giving the difficulty levels a tested baseline.

## Replay the point. Learn from the match.

Want another look at that winner? Pause or resume a replay and choose its playback speed. After the match, a coaching note based on your stats points to something useful to practice next. The final point replay is right there when the match ends.

## More match-day atmosphere, your data stays yours

The serve follow-through reads more clearly, match music has more presence, and referee calls make score changes easier to catch. Court framing and match presentation keep attention on the rally.

Rally Rush keeps your setup and records on your device. You can export a backup, preview and validate a backup before importing it, or deliberately clear local game data. No account or internet connection is needed for local play.

## A quick note before the first serve

Rally Rush remains an offline, single-player game against AI opponents. Version 0.3.0 does not add online multiplayer, accounts, cloud saves, or shared online rankings.
